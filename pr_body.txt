💡 **What:** Use `aria-disabled` instead of `disabled` on the companion reorder buttons in `ProfileUtilitiesSection`, conditionally bypassing the `onClick` handler when disabled, and handling the visual disabled state through conditional CSS classes.
🎯 **Why:** Moving a companion to the first or last position previously disabled the trigger button natively, which caused focus to drop directly to the `<body>` element. This addresses issue #979.
♿ **Accessibility:** Screen reader and keyboard users now keep focus on the button even when it becomes disabled after moving a companion to its final position, preserving context.
