## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility improvements for form elements\n**Learning:** Some form inputs lack explicit placeholders which makes it less clear what to enter without external labels, especially for the profile name field. The tooltip for Relaunch and Launch buttons didn't clearly communicate their disabled state during another active launch.\n**Action:** Add descriptive placeholders for textual inputs. Conditionally update disabled state tooltips and aria-labels to inform users why the buttons are disabled (e.g. 'Another launch in progress').
